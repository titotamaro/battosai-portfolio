Background & Problem Definition

The Problem

Legacy System Constraints
🔒
Desktop-only access. CAPS was a licensed desktop application — no web access, no mobile, no external integration.
✋
Fully manual distribution. All parts data had to be manually captured and converted to PDF or printed as hard copy for dealer distribution.
⏱
Slow pricing & image updates. Price list changes required manual re-entry across separate systems with no single source of truth.
🌐
Web-based microsite. Unlimited access for dealers and parts shops — no license required, fully responsive across devices.
⚡
Centralised data source. Single platform integrating CAPS exports, SAP R3 pricing, and D-NET — eliminating duplicate data entry.
📈
Lead-to-order pipeline. Wishlist and omni-channel features convert parts browsing directly into dealer order opportunities.

User Personas

Who Uses It

4 User Classes
🔧
Parts Shop Admin
Mitsubishi Parts Shop · Jakarta · Tablet & Phone
Needs: Simple app showing parts recommendations based on stock availability
Pain: Hard to locate parts by number quickly; low-to-medium tech literacy
🏢
Dealer Staff
Mitsubishi Dealer · Jakarta · PC Computer
Needs: Find parts by model & vehicle variant; familiar with DMS systems
Pain: Difficult to cross-reference part numbers across vehicle models
🎯
MMKSI After-Sales
MMKSI APM · Jakarta · PC, Tablet & Mobile
Needs: Low-effort CMS for managing & updating master parts data
Pain: High effort to maintain master data across SAP, DMS, and D-NET
🚛
Fleet Customer
Fleet Service Admin · Jakarta · PC & Mobile
Needs: Integrated fleet & vehicle master data with bulk parts ordering
Pain: Managing large fleets with many vehicle types and high parts volume
